Process

A clear path from the first call to a checkup

You always know what happens next. Engagements stay small enough to finish. We write things down so you are not left with a verbal fog of advice.

  1. 01
    Intake conversation
    A 20–30 minute call to learn who lives in the household, what worries you, and whether we are the right fit. No questionnaire maze.
    Typical first call: by appointment, by phone at (949) 666-8823.
  2. 02
    Household assessment
    We review physical routines and the devices, accounts, and services you already use. In person in California when it helps; remote for digital work.
  3. 03
    Written recommendations
    You receive a prioritized plan in plain language: what to do this week, what can wait, and what is not worth the friction.
  4. 04
    Hands-on implementation
    We sit with you (or walk you through screen share) to change settings, install tools, and practice the new habits so they stick.
  5. 05
    Follow-up checkup
    After the dust settles, we reconvene to fix what snagged, answer new questions, and confirm backups and 2FA still work.

What you should prepare

  • A short list of devices in the house, including kids' and guests'.
  • Who currently knows the Wi-Fi password, door codes, and email recovery options.
  • Upcoming travel or household changes that make timing matter.
  • Anything you already tried that did not stick.

Ready for a quieter security posture?

Call for a short intake conversation. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.
