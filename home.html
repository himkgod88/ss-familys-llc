Who we work with

Families first. Professionals who travel. Anyone tired of leftover logins.

SS Family's LLC is a consulting practice, not a monitoring center. If you need 911, call 911. If you need a calmer, specific plan for the house and the phones, that is our work.

  • Households sharing tablets, school portals, and shopping accounts
  • People who travel and want a short, usable briefing—not a binder
  • Parents setting up first phones without handing over the keys
  • Anyone who reused one password for a decade and is ready to stop

Process

Five steps. No mystery.

Full process
  1. 01 Intake conversation

    A 20–30 minute call to learn who lives in the household, what worries you, and whether we are the right fit. No questionnaire maze.

  2. 02 Household assessment

    We review physical routines and the devices, accounts, and services you already use. In person in California when it helps; remote for digital work.

  3. 03 Written recommendations

    You receive a prioritized plan in plain language: what to do this week, what can wait, and what is not worth the friction.

  4. 04 Hands-on implementation

    We sit with you (or walk you through screen share) to change settings, install tools, and practice the new habits so they stick.

  5. 05 Follow-up checkup

    After the dust settles, we reconvene to fix what snagged, answer new questions, and confirm backups and 2FA still work.
